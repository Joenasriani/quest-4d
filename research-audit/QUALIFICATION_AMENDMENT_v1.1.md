**Mathematical correction:** Every original vertex v belongs to {−1,+1}^4 and has Euclidean norm ||v||_2=2. The source's six coordinate-plane rotations are orthogonal, so all reachable rotated vertices preserve that norm. In particular, −2 ≤ w ≤ 2. The implemented perspective denominator 4−w+0.0001 therefore lies in [2.0001,6.0001]. There is no denominator singularity reachable for the project's current 16-vertex model. The added epsilon is not needed to establish this bound. Claims about singularity risk in the current model are withdrawn.
